# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RecruitAgent is used by HR staff, recruiting managers, interviewers, and system administrators. They work inside a data-heavy recruiting workflow: creating jobs, uploading resumes, reviewing AI screening evidence, approving human decisions, and preparing candidate communication.

## Product Purpose

RecruitAgent helps recruiting teams turn job descriptions and resumes into structured evidence, ranked candidates, reviewable screening results, and safer AI-assisted communication drafts. Success means the recruiter can complete the main workflow from job setup to candidate follow-up without losing traceability or human control.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. The interface should feel like a trustworthy operations console rather than a marketing demo. AI appears as an evidence assistant, not as a black-box decision maker.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS landing-page patterns, oversized hero panels, vague AI sparkle copy, dense color gradients, and dashboard cards that show numbers without operational value. Avoid hiding critical review actions behind playful or unfamiliar controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep the recruiter in the workflow: every screen should make the next operational action obvious.
- Show evidence before judgment: AI scores need supporting advantages, risks, missing information, and review state.
- Prefer familiar product patterns: tables, forms, tabs, drawers, and clear actions are strengths here.
- Design for auditability: task status, model calls, prompt versions, and human review state should be easy to inspect.
- Use restrained emphasis: color is for state, selection, and primary actions, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls. Preserve keyboard focus indicators, use visible form labels, avoid color-only status communication, and keep motion short and state-driven with reduced-motion fallbacks.
